About Me.

My Story

I started YouTube in the summer of 2025. I, like most, started with CapCut. I knew nothing about editing, but CapCut was easy enough to learn. I got to 1k subs pretty fast, which drove me to keep posting. However, I took an 8 month break because school was really stressful. In May 2026, I came back and improved my content a lot. Then around July, I finally got AE. At first it was hard to learn, but now I'm I've gotten way better and am still learning to this day.

Thanks to your guys incredible support, I've reach over 11k subscribers and over 10 million overall views. YouTube is my biggest platform, however I do post on TikTok and Instagram.
